## Websites

- A real designed site, not a demo page: hierarchy, spacing, typography, a coherent visual system.
- Meaningful browser-side interaction where it fits: filters, tabs, drawers, galleries, small simulations, local persistence, tasteful motion.
- Typical structure: `index.html` (semantic shell) · `css/styles.css` · `src/main.ts` (bundled to `js/main.js`) + focused `src/<feature>.ts` · `assets/`.
